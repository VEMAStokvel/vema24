Vema24 Privacy Policy

We respect your right to privacy. This policy explains what information we collect for our stokvel savings platform, how we use it, how we protect it, and what your rights are under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Regulatory disclosure

Vema Investment (Pty) Ltd is registered with the National Credit Regulator (NCR) as a credit provider under registration number NCRCP14391. Vema24’s main features relate to stokvel savings. If we offer or administer any product or service that constitutes credit under the National Credit Act 34 of 2005 (NCA), that activity is governed by the NCA and by the specific terms and pre-agreement quotations supplied to you when that product applies.

1. Consent

By using Vema24 or submitting your personal details, you give us your voluntary, specific and informed consent to collect, process and share your personal information as described in this policy.

You can withdraw your consent at any time. Please note, this may affect our ability to provide certain services to you.

2. What Personal Information We Collect

We collect personal information mainly from you. Where the law requires verification or reporting, we may also receive limited information from trusted partners or regulators. This may include:

  • Full name, ID or passport number, and date of birth
  • Contact details (address, phone number, email)
  • Financial and payment details needed for stokvel contributions (for example bank or payment references, proof of payment you upload)
  • Employment or other details if we reasonably need them to operate your membership or meet legal duties
  • Nominated beneficiary details where your stokvel plan or constitution requires them
  • Records of your communication with Vema24 and activity on the platform (for example join requests and contribution logs)

3. How We Use Your Personal Information

Your information is used for the following purposes:

  • Verifying your identity and operating your member account
  • Processing stokvel join requests, contributions, approvals, and member communications
  • Complying with applicable laws (including POPIA, the NCA where credit products apply, and, where relevant, financial crime and tax record-keeping rules)
  • Managing operational and security risk on the platform
  • Keeping accurate membership and contribution records
  • Communicating with you about your plans, payments, constitutions, and service updates
  • Preventing fraud, money laundering, and unlawful activities
  • Sending you relevant information about Vema24 and stokvel plans (with your consent, where required)

4. Direct Marketing

By becoming a member of Vema24, you agree to receive updates and promotional information about stokvel plans and the platform via SMS, WhatsApp, email, or phone.

You may unsubscribe at any time and we will stop sending you marketing content.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We take privacy and security seriously. Your information is protected using:

  • Encrypted databases and secure servers
  • Strict access controls (only authorised staff may view your data)
  • SSL technology for safe online transactions
  • Firewalls and monitoring systems to prevent unauthorised access
  • Regular security checks and compliance testing

6. Sharing of Information

We only share your personal information when necessary, such as with:

  • Banks, payment processors, or technology providers who help us receive or verify contributions
  • Credit bureaus, the NCR, regulatory, tax, or law-enforcement authorities where the NCA or other law requires it
  • Professional advisers or auditors bound by confidentiality, where needed
  • Legal or compliance authorities (if required by law or to protect our rights)

All third parties are legally bound to protect your data and use it only for the purpose required.

7. Your Rights

As a member, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or update any incorrect details
  • Withdraw your consent for direct marketing
  • Request that we delete or restrict the use of your information (where the law allows)
  • Lodge a complaint with us if you believe your privacy rights are not respected

8. Retention of Information

We only keep your personal information for as long as is necessary to:

  • Provide you with services
  • Meet our legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations
  • Resolve disputes

Once this period expires, your information will be safely destroyed or de-identified.

9. Children's Information

If you provide details of a child (for example, as a nominee under a stokvel plan), we will only process that information with the consent of a parent or legal guardian, in line with POPIA.

10. Cookies & Online Information

When you visit our website, we may collect anonymous information such as your IP address, device type, and browsing activity. We use cookies to improve your online experience, analyse website usage, and offer personalised services.

You can disable cookies in your browser, but this may affect site functionality.

11. International Data Transfers

If your personal information is stored or processed outside South Africa (for example, on secure cloud servers), Vema24 will ensure that the same level of legal protection applies as required by POPIA.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on our official platforms.
